Started by Jacob Porter and updated by John Quinlan since 2023, this is the most comprehensive list of Proven Providers in Dorado. Instead of the Excel file that John has regularly shared with you in the past, it now comes in this easier-to-use website version, which was triggered by Christian Kleiner's AI version.

Electricians, appliance repairs, plumbers, AC techs, contractors, doctors, nail salons, restaurants and more, each recommended by a neighbor who used them. Recommendations come from WhatsApp groups (Dorado Beach Community chats, Guys Dinner Group, Ladies Open Forum and more), text messages and personal conversations.

It also has a Watch List of providers that neighbors have had negative experiences with.

About this list

    
Dorado Proven Providers is a free resource for the Dorado community, maintained by John and Katie Quinlan.

    
Wrong number, a provider who's no longer around, or a listing that should come down? Message John and he'll fix it.

These are personal recommendations from Dorado neighbors, gathered from WhatsApp groups, text messages and personal conversations, and lightly edited for clarity. Dorado Proven Providers does not check licenses or insurance and cannot guarantee any provider's work. Ask for a written estimate and agree on the scope before paying a deposit. Watch List entries reflect individual neighbors' experiences, not a verdict on the provider. Call and WhatsApp buttons may not open on every device; the number is always shown so you can copy it.
